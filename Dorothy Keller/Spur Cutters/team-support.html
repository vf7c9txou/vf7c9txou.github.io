Spur Cutters Support

Spur Cutters places a tooth count on one of eight cutter bands. Whole depth is 2.157 divided by the diametral pitch. Tooth thickness at the pitch line is 1.57 divided by the same pitch. The blank diameter is the tooth count plus two, divided by the pitch. Below 12 teeth the saved cutter stays.
