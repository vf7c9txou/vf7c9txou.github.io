Clay Test Bars Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Clay Test Bars stores clay-body names and the plastic, dry, and fired lengths of test bars on this iPhone. It calculates drying shrinkage, firing shrinkage, total shrinkage, and the mold size for a fired size you enter. The app does not require an account and does not include purchases.
